# KeJiShuCloud 3.0 — Product Contract

## Mission
An independent, global technology IP that converts AI research into clear, sourced writing and reproducible software systems.

## Audience
Developers evaluating agent engineering, professionals considering AI productivity workflows, and technical readers looking for practical analysis beyond headlines.

## Core journeys
1. Visitors understand the positioning within the first screen, discover public repositories, and open an original source in two clicks.
2. Readers find a relevant article, evaluate citations, and switch locale without losing context.
3. Readers compare tools by documented workflow purpose and checked source without unverified prices or success claims.
4. Researchers examine experiments and can disable animation or JavaScript without losing site content.

## Governance
No fake stats, paid testimonials, unreviewed automatic publishing, speculative tool rankings, unverifiable biographical claims or green brand colors.

## Quality release gate
TypeScript strict, all approved content valid, static build successful, every locale route and canonical checked, 1440/1024/390/320 browser screenshots reviewed. Performance, accessibility and SEO budgets must be measured before production release.
